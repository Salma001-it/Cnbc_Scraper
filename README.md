# CNBC News Scraper
A Python-based financial news scraping pipeline that collects daily news related to all S&P 500 companies from CNBC.
The pipeline runs daily across all S&P 500 companies and automatically uploads the updated dataset to my Hugging Face profile SelmaNajih001/Cnbc_MultiCompany
<img width="1184" height="827" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f65cc774-a843-4f4b-9270-4e6b72fa155a" />

The project automatically searches company-related articles and extracts structured financial news data for research and analysis purposes.

For each company ticker, the scraper collects:

* Company ticker
* Company name
* Article title
* Publication date
* Article URL
* Article content
